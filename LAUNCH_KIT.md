## 1. Product Hunt

**Name:** PuffBreak — Your Digital Break Room
**Tagline:** A mindful 3-minute virtual break — light a digital cigarette or sip chai, without the smoke.
**Category:** Health & Fitness / Wellness / Productivity

**Description (first post):**
> PuffBreak is a free, anonymous, browser-based virtual break room. You light a virtual cigarette (lighter or match, realistic smoke physics) or sip a cup of chai in one of 8 immersive ambient rooms — office rooftop, beach sunset, space station, library, park bench, metro platform, chai stall, or pure silence.
>
> It was built as the English-language answer to Korea's viral "online damta" (담타) trend — a real cultural moment where office workers take their smoke break online. PuffBreak adds ASMR audio mixing, chai mode, stealth/zen modes, anonymous chat, and a PWA.
>
> Why? Because a "break" shouldn't mean doom-scrolling. It should be 180 seconds of calm.
>
> **No sign-up. No ads. No data collection.** Funded by the community.

**First comment (seeded):**

>
> PuffBreak is a free, anonymous, browser-based break room:
> - Light a virtual cigarette (lighter/match ignition, physics smoke) or sip virtual chai

> - Anonymous chat, real-time presence, streak tracking

>
> No accounts, no ads, no tracking. It's a solo project; the community keeps it alive.
